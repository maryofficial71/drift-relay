# Drift-Relay

Drift-Relay is a decentralized shipping verification network that creates immutable proof-of-transit records by automatically capturing IoT sensor data, GPS coordinates, and biometric signatures from stakeholders across the supply chain. Unlike traditional blockchain supply chain solutions that depend on manual data entry, this system continuously monitors shipment conditions including temperature, humidity, shock, and tamper detection to provide real-time validation. The platform generates tamper-proof digital passports for each shipment containing complete provenance data and compliance certificates.

The system implements a novel Proof-of-Transit consensus mechanism where logistics participants including carriers, warehouses, customs officials, and receivers serve as network validators by providing cryptographic signatures and sensor data. Smart contracts automatically execute compliance checks against predefined shipping requirements and trigger payments, insurance claims, or alerts when conditions are breached. The platform features a unique drift detection algorithm that identifies route or condition anomalies by comparing real-time data against historical patterns and machine learning models, while supporting multiple blockchain networks through a relay architecture for cross-chain international shipment verification.

Drift-Relay addresses critical pain points in global trade including cargo theft, counterfeit goods, regulatory compliance failures, and insurance fraud by providing real-time visibility across complex multi-modal transportation networks. The solution offers predictive analytics for delivery optimization, automated customs documentation, and dynamic insurance premium adjustment based on continuous risk assessment. Advanced integration capabilities with existing ERP systems through standardized APIs enable seamless adoption while maintaining complete transparency and trust across all stakeholders in the supply chain.
